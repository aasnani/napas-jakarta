## Instrument identity and status

Peraturan Pemerintah Nomor 22 Tahun 2021 is titled Penyelenggaraan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup. The BPK database describes it as a national environmental regulation and records the subject areas that include environmental approval, air-quality protection and management, environmental information, supervision and administrative sanctions.

The BPK record identifies the legal basis as the Constitution, Undang-Undang Nomor 32 Tahun 2009 and Undang-Undang Nomor 11 Tahun 2020. The record should be read together with later amendments and implementing instruments; this source entry does not claim that every later provision is unchanged.

## Ambient-air provisions

The BPK abstract states that the regulation includes protection and management of air quality. The official PDF search extract identifies a provision that ambient-air quality standards are listed in Lampiran VII and that those standards are used as the basis for the highest ambient concentration in a designated air-quality protection and management area.

The lampiran is the controlling locator for numeric ambient standards. A response must not substitute an ISPU category for an ambient concentration standard, and must identify the pollutant and averaging time before comparing a measurement.

## Supervision and sanctions

The BPK abstract describes supervision and law enforcement as mechanisms intended to ensure that obligations in environmental approvals and business permits are carried out as planned. It also describes administrative sanctions for deviations.

This is a rule-on-paper source. It does not establish that Jakarta inspected every regulated source, that a sanction was imposed in a particular case, or that implementation changed a measured concentration. Those claims require dated implementation records.
