# Jakarta air-pollution citizen lawsuit: what the court record establishes

Published: decision dated 2021-09-16; directory accessed 2026-09-07

Jurisdiction: Indonesia, DKI Jakarta, Banten, and West Java parties in the case

Tags: topic=regulation-implementation; audience=public-accountability; action=participation

The Supreme Court case directory records Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat case
374/Pdt.G/LH/2019/PN Jkt.Pst, a citizen lawsuit concerning Jakarta air
pollution, with a decision dated 16 September 2021. The record identifies
government defendants and surrounding-province parties. A court finding about
public duties and regional coordination is legal evidence, not proof that each
ordered action has been implemented or that a later air-quality value was
caused by non-compliance.

Answers should report the case number and decision date, distinguish findings
from orders, and verify appeal/execution status from the official case record
before claiming completion. [jakarta-court-air-quality]
