## Instrument identity

Peraturan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan Nomor 8 Tahun 2023 concerns the application of emission standards for motor vehicles in categories M, N, O and L. The BPK record lists it as an environmental and land/sea/air transport regulation, in Indonesian, and records the status as Berlaku.

The record gives the adoption date as 4 August 2023, promulgation date as 14 August 2023, and effective date as 14 August 2023. It identifies the source as Berita Negara Tahun 2023 Nomor 624 and notes an eight-page instrument plus a fifteen-page attachment.

## Covered vehicles and obligation

The official abstract says that the regulation applies to categories M, N, O and L that have been in service for more than three years. It states that vehicle owners must meet the applicable emission standard and that the instrument regulates both the application of emission standards and emission testing.

The abstract identifies testing through periodic-test implementing units and emission-test implementing units. A question about a vehicle in Jakarta therefore needs the vehicle category, age and testing route before a precise legal conclusion is made.

## Supersession and limits

The BPK status section records that this regulation revokes Minister of Environment Regulation Number 05 of 2006 on old motor-vehicle exhaust-gas thresholds. A legal answer should cite the current instrument and its effective date rather than relying on the revoked rule.

The BPK page is evidence of the instrument's identity, scope and status. It does not provide a denominator for Jakarta testing coverage or proof that all eligible vehicles complied. Those implementation claims need DKI enforcement records and dated statistics.
