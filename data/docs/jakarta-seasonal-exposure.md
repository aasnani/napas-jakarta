# Jakarta timing and local exposure context

DLH DKI, BMKG, KLHK, and Dinas Kesehatan warned that PM2.5 can increase in
the dry season (roughly May–August in the notice) when rain and wind are low,
because particles can accumulate and remain suspended. The notice also says
SPKU observations showed different day/night patterns. These are reasons to
check the current station timestamp before planning exercise; they are not a
rule that morning is always safer or that every district follows the same
pattern.

The same notice identifies both local sources (including transport and
residential activity) and regional industrial sources. A resident can lower
dose by choosing a cleaner time and route, reducing intense exertion near
traffic, and moving activity indoors when the current reading is high. A
station is not a personal monitor: use the nearest relevant station and state
the time and location when making a decision. [jakarta-seasonal-exposure]
