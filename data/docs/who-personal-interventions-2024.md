## Purpose

The WHO Q&A addresses personal interventions and risk communication for air pollution. It presents exposure-reduction options as public-health advice and does not replace emission-control policy or clinical care.

## Exposure reduction

The guidance emphasizes checking air-quality information and reducing exposure during pollution episodes, including adjusting strenuous outdoor activity when conditions are poor. The appropriate action depends on the pollutant, duration, activity and vulnerability of the person.

Indoor measures can reduce exposure when outdoor air is polluted, but indoor sources and ventilation conditions matter. A room or cleaner should not be described as automatically safe without considering filtration, airflow, maintenance, occupancy and indoor combustion.

## Respirator boundary

Particulate respirators can reduce inhaled particles when they are appropriate for the pollutant and fit well. They do not filter every gas, fit varies, and they are not a substitute for reducing emissions. Advice for people with heart or lung disease should be individualized by a clinician.

## Risk communication

The Q&A is a general communication source, not a diagnosis tool. An assistant should state the date and limits of the advice, distinguish population guidance from individual medical advice, and escalate severe symptoms to appropriate urgent care.
