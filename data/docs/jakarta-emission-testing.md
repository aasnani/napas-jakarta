# Jakarta vehicle-emissions testing obligation
Publisher: Dinas Lingkungan Hidup DKI Jakarta
Source: https://lingkunganhidup.jakarta.go.id/faq/ujiemisi
Published: page accessed 2026-09-07 (FAQ date not stated)
Status as of: 2026-09-07; Pergub 66/2020 is listed as berlaku
Jurisdiction: DKI Jakarta
Document type: official_faq
Tags: topic=regulation-implementation; audience=vehicle-owner; action=compliance

DLH DKI's FAQ says emissions testing is mandatory for individual passenger
cars and motorcycles older than three years under Pergub 66/2020. It also
describes a traffic-ticket consequence for vehicles that fail, under the
national traffic-law framework. The rule, a test result, a roadside inspection,
and an actual sanction are different events; a legal obligation alone does not
show the share of vehicles tested or repaired.

For accountability, publish the eligible-vehicle denominator, test-centre
capacity and calibration, pass/fail and retest counts, roadside-check coverage,
and sanction follow-through by period. No complete denominator or independent
compliance rate is asserted here because it is not supplied by this FAQ.
