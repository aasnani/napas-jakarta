## Scope

The EPA guide discusses portable air cleaners, heating/ventilation and air-conditioning filters, indoor pollutant sources and practical limits. It is technical household guidance and should not be presented as a Jakarta regulation.

## CADR and room size

The guide explains that a portable cleaner's clean air delivery rate (CADR) and the room size determine how much filtered air is delivered. A product recommendation therefore needs room dimensions and a stated particle CADR, rather than only a marketing label.

## HVAC filtration

The guide discusses higher-efficiency HVAC filters, including MERV 13 where the system can accommodate the pressure drop. Compatibility, fit, operating time and maintenance affect delivered filtration; a filter rating alone does not guarantee a measured indoor concentration.

## Indoor sources and cautions

The guide also identifies indoor sources and notes that filtration does not remove every pollutant. Smoking, cooking, incense, candles and other combustion sources should be reduced or controlled, while ventilation decisions should account for outdoor conditions.
