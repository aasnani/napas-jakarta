# Ways to improve Jakarta air quality
Publisher: Pemerintah Provinsi DKI Jakarta / Dinas Lingkungan Hidup
Source: https://www.jakarta.go.id/page/upaya-bersama-jaga-kualitas-udara-jakarta

Jakarta's air-pollution strategy combines governance and monitoring, reduction
of emissions from mobile sources, and reduction from stationary sources. The
intervention set includes public transport and vehicle-emission testing,
low-emission zones, cleaner energy and fuels, industrial and power-plant
monitoring, construction-dust control, open-burning and waste controls, green
infrastructure, and better public disclosure.

The city also promotes low-emission mobility such as public transport, walking,
and cycling when conditions are suitable. These actions complement—rather than
replace—regulation and enforcement of industrial, energy, transport, and waste
sources. A modelled reduction or policy target must not be presented as an
observed reduction unless a suitable evaluation measured it.
