# Reducing personal exposure to air pollution
Publisher: World Health Organization
Source: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240000278

People can reduce exposure by checking a fresh local observation, changing the
time or location of strenuous outdoor activity, and reducing intense exercise
during high-pollution episodes. Staying indoors helps only when indoor sources
are controlled and outdoor particles are not being drawn inside.

A properly fitted, certified particulate respirator can reduce inhaled
particles such as PM2.5, but it does not remove every gas and fit is important.
Portable particle filtration may reduce indoor PM2.5, but effectiveness depends
on room size, filter performance, fan speed, infiltration, maintenance, cost,
and indoor sources. People with relevant medical conditions should seek advice
from a qualified clinician. This information supports exposure decisions and
does not diagnose an individual.
