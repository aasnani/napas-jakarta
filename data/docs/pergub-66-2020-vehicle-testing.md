## Instrument identity and dates

Peraturan Gubernur Provinsi DKI Jakarta Nomor 66 Tahun 2020 is titled Uji Emisi Gas Buang Kendaraan Bermotor. The BPK record gives the adoption date as 22 July 2020, promulgation date as 24 July 2020, and effective date as 24 December 2020.

The BPK abstract explains that the regulation replaced Pergub Nomor 92 Tahun 2007 so that Jakarta's rules could align with the national traffic and regional-government framework. The record marks the instrument as a provincial regulation and records thirteen pages.

## Scope and administration

The abstract says the regulation governs emission testing and exhaust gas by every motor-vehicle owner, together with inspection, supervision and control by the emission-test team. Its delayed effective date is six months after promulgation, rather than the promulgation date itself.

This rule-on-paper description does not show how many vehicles were tested, passed or failed, where enforcement occurred, or how sanctions were followed through. Those are separate implementation questions and must be supported by DKI records with a date and denominator.

## Legal interpretation boundary

The BPK record is a primary legal database entry and reports status as Berlaku. It is not a substitute for checking the official consolidated instrument, implementing notices, or later amendments before giving advice about a particular vehicle or ticket.
