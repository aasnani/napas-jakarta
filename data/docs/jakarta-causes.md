# What causes Jakarta air pollution
Publisher: Dinas Lingkungan Hidup DKI Jakarta / Vital Strategies / ITB
Source: https://rendahemisi.jakarta.go.id/learn

Jakarta's pollution has multiple local and regional sources. The 2020 DKI
emissions inventory attributed major shares of NOx, CO, PM10, and PM2.5
emissions to transport, while manufacturing was the largest source of SO2 and
a major secondary source for particulate pollution. These are emissions-
inventory estimates, not a direct measurement of a person's exposure.

The 2019–2020 Jakarta/ITB/Vital Strategies receptor-based source-apportionment
study found that transport contributed substantially to ambient PM2.5, with
different estimates by season and sampling site. It also identified coal
combustion, construction, open burning, soil, and road dust. Emissions
inventory and receptor apportionment answer different questions and should not
be collapsed into one universal percentage. Wind, rainfall, atmospheric mixing,
season, and sources outside DKI can change observed concentrations.

The evidence supports statements about likely contributors, not a claim that a
single source caused one station's reading. Current station observations are
descriptive and should be interpreted with their timestamp, location, and
quality metadata.
