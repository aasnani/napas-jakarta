DLH describes continuous emissions monitoring systems (CEMS) as a way to
measure and calculate emissions released from stacks in near real time. Its
public monitoring page lists operational-control and evaluation reports for
stationary sources for 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024, including a 2024
iron-and-steel smelting CEMS item.

The existence of reports demonstrates monitoring activity, not that every
industrial stack is covered, every reading is compliant, or corrective orders
were completed. A useful implementation dashboard should publish the regulated
source denominator, uptime and missingness, exceedances, inspections, orders,
sanctions, and closure dates. [jakarta-cems-monitoring]
