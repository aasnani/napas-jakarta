# WHO air-quality guideline context
Publisher: World Health Organization
Source: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240034228

WHO air-quality guideline levels are health-based recommendations for pollutants
including PM2.5, PM10, ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide, and carbon
monoxide. They are not Indonesian legal standards. Answers should state which
framework is being used when comparing a measurement with a threshold.
