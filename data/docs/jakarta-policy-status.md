# Policy proposals and implementation gaps
Publisher: Pemerintah Provinsi DKI Jakarta / JDIH
Source: https://www.jakarta.go.id/siaran-pers/3850-SP-HMS-01-2023

Electronic Road Pricing (PL2SE/ERP) is a policy proposal and legislative
process, not automatically an enacted operating rule. Jakarta's official
explanation records that its draft first entered the legislative programme in
2018 but was not discussed, was revised and resubmitted, and required a strong
regional legal basis before implementation. A later DKI Environmental Agency
status update described a traffic-management draft still covering ERP, stronger
Low Emission Zones, and progressive parking measures.

The assistant must distinguish a draft, a pilot, a binding regulation with
weak enforcement, and a court order awaiting implementation. For each policy
status answer, report the status as of a date, quote or paraphrase documented
procedural reasons, and label stakeholder criticism or causal interpretation as
interpretation rather than fact. Status can change and must be rechecked from
the legal repository.

The Jakarta citizen lawsuit and later court decisions are evidence about public
duties and legal findings, not proof that every recommended intervention has
been implemented.
