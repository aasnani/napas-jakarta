# Jakarta and Indonesian air-quality regulations
Publisher: JDIH DKI Jakarta / Government of Indonesia
Source: https://udara.jakarta.go.id/regulasi

Permen LHK No. 14 of 2020 defines the Indonesian ISPU methodology and
categories. ISPU is a unitless index and is not a pollutant concentration.
PP No. 22 of 2021 establishes national environmental and ambient air-quality
provisions. Pergub DKI No. 66 of 2020 regulates motor-vehicle emissions
testing. Ingub DKI No. 66 of 2019 and Kepgub DKI No. 576 of 2023 direct or
organize Jakarta air-pollution control actions.

These instruments have different legal forms and authorities. A WHO guideline
is health-based advice, not Indonesian legislation. A strategy or governor's
instruction must not be described as the same thing as a statute or binding
ambient standard. Answers should identify the instrument, jurisdiction,
effective status, relevant date, and source article before summarizing it.
