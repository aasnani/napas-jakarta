## Source identity

The official BPK download URL identifies a 2021 Ministerial Environment and Forestry regulation. The PDF search extract exposes a CEMS connectivity test table with fields for stack code, emission source, CEMS brand, parameters and pass/fail connectivity result.

## CEMS evidence boundary

CEMS connectivity or data transmission is not itself proof that an emission source met every applicable emission limit. A grounded answer should separate installation, connectivity, data completeness, quality assurance, limit compliance and enforcement follow-up.

## Coverage denominator

Any CEMS compliance percentage needs the number of sources required to monitor under the applicable approval or regulation and the number actually monitored. A public list of connected instruments without the required-source denominator cannot establish complete coverage.
