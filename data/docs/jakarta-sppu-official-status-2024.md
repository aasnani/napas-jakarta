## Status and scope

The 19 June 2024 DKI statement is implementation evidence about air-quality management and the SPPU context. It is not an enacted statute or an ambient standard. Its date must remain attached to claims about what the administration reported.

## Workstreams

The official statement discusses work involving emissions inventories, stronger oversight of mobile and stationary sources, and cooperation across the Greater Jakarta agglomeration. These are documented workstreams and should not be rewritten as completed outcomes without separate results.

## Evidence gap

A statement describing an inventory or oversight program does not provide a complete denominator for all eligible vehicles, regulated stacks, inspections, sanctions, or CEMS follow-up. A reviewer should request the underlying dated datasets, methodology, coverage and quality controls.

## Interpretation

The statement supports a cautious distinction between a policy activity and a measured air-quality outcome. Transported pollution, multiple authorities and incomplete public denominators are plausible constraints to investigate; they do not prove that one policy or source caused a particular reading.
