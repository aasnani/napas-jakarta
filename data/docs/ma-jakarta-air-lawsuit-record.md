## Record identity

The Mahkamah Agung court portal is the authoritative starting point for locating Indonesian court records. The Jakarta air-pollution citizen lawsuit concerns duties across Jakarta and the surrounding regional authorities; a court-profile URL alone is not the complete judgment.

## Judgment versus implementation

A judgment or order can establish what a court directed or declared. It does not by itself establish that every direction was implemented, funded, monitored or completed. Those claims require subsequent official execution, government-response or monitoring records with dates.

## Regional coordination

Questions about Jakarta's airshed should preserve the jurisdictions named in the case record. Pollution transport and shared authorities are reasons to investigate regional coordination; they are not proof that a particular source caused a particular observation.
