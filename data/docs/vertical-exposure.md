# High-rise and ground-level exposure
Publisher: Research literature; geographic scope must be checked per study
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/15/8/1319

Some street-canyon studies observe lower particulate concentrations at greater
heights, but the relationship is not universal. Road distance, building and
street geometry, wind, nearby stacks, window opening, ventilation, filtration,
indoor sources, and time spent outdoors can dominate the result.

Evidence from another city or building cannot automatically be generalized to
Jakarta. A rooftop monitoring station is not the same as pedestrian-level
exposure, and a high apartment floor is not automatically safer. Answers must
state the study location, measurement height, indoor/outdoor setting, pollutant,
and limitations. Exact sensor height should only be reported when official
station metadata supplies it.
